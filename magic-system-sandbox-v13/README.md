# Magic System Sandbox v13 — Living Civilization

This is the major civilization-simulation expansion.

## Regions
A civilization now begins with regions such as a capital, agricultural provinces, frontier territory, research districts, and potentially industrial/trade regions. Each region tracks population, magical development, stability, wealth, and a dominant Spell Class.

## Institutions
Spell Classes can generate persistent academies and other institutions. Institutions have members, influence, founding year, focus, and a home region.

## Research
Research projects emerge from the actual magic graph:
- spell theory
- rule-boundary studies
- material/tool engineering
Projects advance over simulated years, complete, affect Knowledge/Technology, and become persistent discoveries.

## Technology and professions
The simulation tracks a Technology stat and derives professions from the graph, such as:
- <Spell Class> Specialist
- Arcane Engineer
- Magical Material Worker
- Rule Theorist
- Technique Instructor

## Event chains and feedback
Civilization events depend on both the magic graph and the current civilization state. Examples:
- rule exceptions can produce exploit research
- materials can create industries, shortages, and trade
- tools can create industrial adoption and accidents
- high Arcane Risk enables major safety regulation
- low Stability creates unrest
- high Knowledge unlocks advanced spell breakthroughs
- Spell Classes create institutions, regional specialization, and academic rivalry

## Event Inspector
Click any event to see:
- why it happened
- which magic concepts/state thresholds contributed
- its numerical consequences

## Civilization Graph
A second graph grows from the simulation, containing:
- the civilization
- regions
- institutions
- industries/infrastructure
- discoveries
and the relationships between them.

## Time controls
- +1 Year
- +10 Years
- +50 Years
- Auto simulation / Pause

Quiet years can pass without producing a major event.

## Reset Magic System
The new Reset toolbar button asks for confirmation, then removes every spell, rule, material, tool, technique, principle, and connection and returns the project to a single glowing MANA node.
